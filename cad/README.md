# CAD Design

This folder documents the mechanical design process of the **Autonomous Mecanum Rover**. Rather than showing only the final design, it captures the major design iterations, the engineering challenges encountered, and the reasoning behind each redesign.

## Design Iterations

- [Iteration 1 – Initial Chassis Expansion](iteration1.md)
- [Iteration 2 – Hybrid Aluminum Chassis Redesign](iteration2.md)
- [Iteration 3 – Simulation-Oriented Redesign](iteration3.md)

Each iteration includes CAD models, design decisions, fabrication challenges, and lessons learned that influenced the next version of the rover.
